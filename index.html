A Human Story

Behind Every Number
Is a Person

"He was my son first, before the law made him a criminal."

Thousands of individuals in Malaysia face the ultimate, irreversible punishment — many for non-violent drug offences — under laws that strip judges of their ability to consider individual circumstances, background, or rehabilitation.

Families are torn apart. No second chances. No possibility of mercy. This is not justice — it is a system that fails the very people it claims to protect.

The Petition

We Demand Change.
Sign Today.

This petition calls on the Malaysian Government and relevant authorities to take immediate, concrete action.

  • 01Fully abolish mandatory death sentencing for all drug offences with immediate effect
  • 02Conduct a comprehensive review of all current death row cases for resentencing
  • 03Grant judges full discretion to consider individual circumstances in all capital cases
  • 04Establish an independent oversight body for capital punishment review
  • 05Ratify the Second Optional Protocol to the ICCPR toward full abolition

This petition will be delivered to the Malaysian Ministry of Law, the Human Rights Commission of Malaysia (SUHAKAM), and the United Nations Special Rapporteur on Extrajudicial Executions.
